## 2. Measured Evaluation Results (Temporal Test Split, Post-Calibration)

Temporal splits with `enforce_label_maturity: true`:
- **Train** (`<= 2025-03-07T00:00:00Z`): `1,556` matured transactions (`63` positives, `381` immature delayed labels excluded)
- **Validation** (`<= 2025-04-06T00:00:00Z`): `516` matured transactions (`30` positives, `428` immature delayed labels excluded)
- **Test** (`<= 2025-05-01T00:00:00Z`): `708` transactions (`29` positives, `4.10%` fraud rate)

| Model | Features | Test PR-AUC | Δ PR-AUC vs Tabular | Recall @ 1% FPR | Recall @ 5% FPR | Raw ECE → Cal ECE | Cal Brier | Fraud Ring Det. (@0.35) | Legit Shared Device FPR (@0.35) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `logistic_tabular_baseline` | 10 (no graph) | `0.7274` | `0.0000` | `0.5172` | `0.9310` | `0.1684 → 0.0179` | `0.0149` | `1.0000` | `0.0000` |
| `logistic` (graph) | 20 (graph) | `0.8558` | `+0.1284` | `0.8276` | `0.9310` | `0.1334 → 0.0184` | `0.0133` | `0.9259` | `0.0000` |
| `boosted` (HistGBDT) | 20 (graph) | **`0.8955`** | **`+0.1681`** | `0.8621` | `0.9310` | `0.0228 → 0.0079` | **`0.0095`** | `0.9259` | `0.0000` |
| `gnn` (2-layer TemporalGraphSAGE) | 20 + 2-hop MP | `0.8428` | `+0.1153` | **`0.8966`** | `0.9310` | `0.0519 → 0.0182` | `0.0118` | `0.9630` | `0.0086` |

### Key Observations
1. **Graph feature lift**: Adding strictly causal temporal graph features boosts PR-AUC by `+0.1153` to `+0.1681` and boosts Recall @ 1% FPR from `51.7%` (tabular-only) up to `89.7%` (TemporalGraphSAGE GNN).
2. **Calibration**: Platt scaling fitted strictly on the temporal validation split reduces Expected Calibration Error (ECE) on every model (e.g., `0.1334 → 0.0184` on graph logistic, `0.0228 → 0.0079` on boosted, `0.0519 → 0.0182` on GNN) and lowers Brier score relative to the tabular baseline.
3. **Legitimate shared devices vs coordinated fraud rings**: All graph models achieve `92.6%–96.3%` detection on synthetic fraud rings while holding false-positive rates on legitimate shared-device households to `0.0%–0.86%`.
